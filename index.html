04

About

Fuel-Film is led by Jon Feuell, a videographer, editor, 3D animator and motion graphics specialist.

On set, Jon has worked on BBC productions including The Great British Sewing Bee and operated ringside cameras for Matchroom Boxing. Time spent at a film equipment rental company means he knows the kit inside out, and he holds a First Class Honours degree in Film and TV Production.

In post, the work runs from corporate and event edits to animated travel adverts, 3D technical breakdowns for engineering firms, and maps and animations for the YouTube channels Mossy Earth and Echo.

For bigger productions, Jon brings in trusted freelance crew, so every project gets a team sized to the job.
