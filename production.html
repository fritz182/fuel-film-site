07

Questions

Where are you based?

London and Bristol, and we travel across the UK and abroad. Travel and accommodation outside those areas are added to the quote.

How quickly can you deliver?

Event highlights can be ready the next day. Brand films usually take two to four weeks from the shoot, depending on feedback rounds.

How many changes can we make?

Feedback rounds are built in, and reasonable changes are fine until the film is right. Changes that go beyond the agreed brief are quoted before any work is done.

Do we get the raw footage and project files?

You get the finished films in every format agreed. Raw footage and project files can be supplied for an extra fee, so ask when you brief us.

How much space does a podcast session need?

A meeting room big enough for a table and two to four chairs is plenty. We bring everything else, and set up in about an hour.
